# Hopped Support

Hopped is a homebrewing companion for iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

## Get help

Before reporting a problem:

1. Update Hopped to the newest version available to you.
2. Restart Hopped and try the action again.
3. Note your device model, operating-system version, Hopped version, and the steps that
   reproduce the problem.

To request help or report a bug, email [Hopped support][email-support].

Do not include recipes, brewing records, Apple Account details, or other information you
want to keep private. Hopped can copy or export a diagnostics report from
**Settings → Storage & Sync → Diagnostics**; review it before attaching any part of it to
a support message.

For details about data handling, read the
[Hopped Privacy Policy](https://github.com/gigq/gigq/blob/main/hopped/PRIVACY.md).
